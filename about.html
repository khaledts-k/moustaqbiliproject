Architectural Mission Brief

Unifying Kuwait's Higher Education Ecosystem

Replacing fragmented administrative sources with a single, authoritative digital clearinghouse covering foreign and domestic scholarships.

!

The Fragmentation Challenge

Prior to this unified platform, Kuwaiti applicants faced substantial information friction across independent channels:

  • Site X (MoHE Central Portal): Published voluminous static PDF decrees without interactive filtering.
  • Site Y (Dispersed Cultural Missions): Washington, London, Dublin, Ottawa, and Canberra published admission deadlines and living salaries on separated websites.
  • Site Z (NBAQ Accreditation Register): Managed the prestigious Merit Universities list independently from quota pools.
✓

The Centralized Solution

This prototype consolidates every administrative dependency into an integrated ministerial engine:

  • Living Salaries & Merit Breakdown: Direct transparency on monthly allowances (Standard vs +50% Merit Bonus).
  • Exam & Visa Clarity: Guidance on SAT/ACT, UCAT, LNAT, and UKVI IELTS vs Standard IELTS.
  • School Grad Split: Detailed thresholds for Kuwait Public Schools, American Diploma GPAs, and British A-Levels.
  • Domestic Comparison: Full admission criteria for Kuwait University, AASU, and Private PUC Scholarships (AUM, GUST, AUK).
