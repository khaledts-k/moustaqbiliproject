About this site

A free guide for students about scholarships and university admission in Kuwait.

The problem

Scholarship information was spread over many websites and long PDF files:

  • The Ministry's scholarship plan is a long PDF file.
  • Each cultural office has its own website.
  • Approved universities are on separate lists.

What this site does

It puts the important parts in one place, in simple words:

  • Monthly allowances, with a currency calculator.
  • The grades, tests and papers you need for each program.
  • Qudrat dates and a score calculator.
  • Answers to common questions, and a way to ask your own.
This site is not part of the Ministry of Higher Education. Always check important decisions with the Ministry.
