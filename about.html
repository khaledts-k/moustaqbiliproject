About Moustaqbili

One Place for All Your University Questions

Bringing together foreign and local scholarship information into one simple portal.

The Challenge

Kuwaiti students previously had to check multiple websites and long PDF documents to get answers:

  • Static MoHE PDF scholarship booklets.
  • Separate websites for each Cultural Office (London, DC, Dublin, Australia).
  • Independent university accreditation lists.

The Solution

Moustaqbili puts everything in one easy-to-use place:

  • Monthly Stipends & Merit Bonus (+50%): Clear allowance details.
  • Exams & Requirements: Details on SAT, IELTS, UCAT, and visas.
  • GPA & Grade Conversions: Easy grade calculators for US, Thanawiya, and A-Levels.
  • Local Universities: Rules for KU, AASU, and Private PUC scholarships.
