Go to the search

The suggested way

  1. Search: pick your country, grade 12 % and level, plus a major if you know it.
  2. Read your results step by step: major, country, what you need, then the money.
  3. Tap a card for its details. "Show everything" opens them all, and "More info" on the side has the extras.
  4. Next: book Qudrat if your major needs it, then follow the steps on "How to Apply".
How to read your results
  • The % is the lowest high school % the plan accepts. A higher % gives you a better chance, but no guarantee.
  • Money is per month. The Ministry sets the amount in the local currency; the Kuwaiti Dinar amount moves a little with the exchange rate.
  • "Merit" means a top university on the Ministry's list: 50% more each month, after the foundation year.
  • "Not confirmed" or "Not checked yet" means: check with the Ministry before you act.
  • No results? Press "Why no results?" to see what happened and what to try.
