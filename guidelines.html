Scholarship Regulations & FAQ

Key rules, language requirements, grade conversions, and visa procedures.

1 UKVI IELTS vs Standard IELTS (ضوابط اختبارات اللغة للفيزا)

Important Visa Rule

Understanding when UKVI IELTS is required vs standard IELTS:

UKVI Academic IELTS is Mandatory for Foundation:

If enrolling in any International Foundation Programme (IFP) or pre-degree course in the UK, UKVI Academic IELTS is strictly required by UK Immigration law.

Standard IELTS will lead to automatic UK visa rejection for foundation.
Standard Academic IELTS / TOEFL for Direct Entry:

For Direct Degree Entry (Year 1 Undergraduate) at UK universities, Standard Academic IELTS or TOEFL is accepted.

Recommendation: Take UKVI Academic IELTS to cover both pathways.

2 Direct Entry vs. Foundation Year

Kuwait high school (Thanawiya) is 12 years, while UK high school is 13 years. Here is how entry works:

Kuwait Thanawiya (Public School) Pathway:

Requires a 1-year Foundation course followed by the 3-year Bachelor degree (total 4 years).

British Curriculum (A-Levels) Pathway:

Students with qualifying A-Levels enter directly into Year 1 of the 3-year Bachelor degree.

United States Pathway:

US universities are 4-year direct entry degrees without a separate foundation year.

4 Visas & Financial Guarantee (الكتاب المالي)

MoHE provides official Financial Guarantee letters for student visa processing:

  • United States (F-1 Visa): Submit I-20, DS-160, SEVIS receipt, and MoHE Financial Guarantee.
  • United Kingdom (Student Visa): Submit CAS, UKVI TB test, and MoHE Financial Guarantee.
  • Canada (Study Permit): Submit Letter of Acceptance (LOA), PAL, and MoHE Guarantee.
  • Australia (Subclass 500): Submit eCoE and OSHC health coverage.
