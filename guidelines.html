Kuwait MoHE Foreign Scholarship Regulations

Official statutory criteria governing student dispatch, testing rules, curriculum splits, and student visa sponsorship.

1 UKVI Academic IELTS vs Standard Academic IELTS (ضوابط اختبارات اللغة للفيزا)

Crucial Immigration Rule

Under UK Visas & Immigration (UKVI) regulations and Kuwait Cultural Attaché guidelines, the distinction between standard and UKVI IELTS is legally binding depending on your entry level:

When UKVI Academic IELTS is MANDATORY:

If enrolling in any course below bachelor's degree level (RQF Level 3 or 4), including International Foundation Programmes (IFP), Pre-Med Foundation, or Pre-sessional English courses, a UKVI Academic IELTS (SELT with a valid URN number) is STRICTLY REQUIRED BY UK LAW.

Standard IELTS will lead to automatic UK visa rejection for foundation.
When Standard Academic IELTS / TOEFL is Sufficient:

For Direct Degree Entry (Year 1 Undergraduate) at UK universities holding Student Sponsor Status (degree level and above), the university may assess your English using Standard Academic IELTS or TOEFL iBT.

MoHE Recommendation: Take UKVI Academic IELTS to cover both pathways.

2 Direct Entry vs. Foundation Year for UK & Commonwealth Schools

In the United Kingdom, undergraduate degrees are 3 years in length (4 years in Scotland). Because the Kuwait General Secondary Certificate (Thanawiya) represents 12 years of schooling compared to the 13-year British pre-university system:

Kuwait Thanawiya (Public School) Pathway:

Students must complete an approved 1-year International Foundation Programme (IFP / Year 0) sponsored by MoHE, followed by the 3-year bachelor's degree (total 4 years).

British Curriculum (GCE A-Levels) Pathway:

Students with 3 qualifying A-Levels (e.g. A*AA, AAA, or ABB) bypass the foundation year and enter directly into Year 1 of the 3-year bachelor's degree.

United States Pathway:

All US universities are 4-year direct entry degrees without separate foundation years. Freshman year covers general education sequences.

4 Student Visa Protocols & The Ministerial Financial Guarantee (الكتاب المالي)

All Kuwait MoHE scholars receive an official government Financial Support Guarantee letter issued by the supervising Cultural Bureau (e.g. Washington DC, London, Dublin). Key embassy requirements:

  • United States (F-1 Visa): Submit the I-20, Form DS-160, SEVIS fee receipt ($350), and the MoHE Financial Guarantee to waive personal bank statements.
  • United Kingdom (Student Visa): Submit the CAS, UKVI TB clearance certificate from an authorized Kuwait clinic, and the embassy financial sponsor letter.
  • Canada (Study Permit): Submit Letter of Acceptance (LOA), Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL), and MoHE guarantee.
  • Australia (Subclass 500): Electronic Confirmation of Enrolment (eCoE) and OSHC policy confirmation.
