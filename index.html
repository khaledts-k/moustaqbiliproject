Every scholarship in the Ministry's 2026/2027 plan is for high-school graduates, so master's and doctorate scholarships are not offered.

Prefer to read? See the steps
  1. Pick a country and the range your grade 12 % falls in.
  2. Level of education: choose "Bachelor's degree", or "Medical or dental degree" for medicine. Master's and PhD aren't offered.
  3. Pick a major if you know it, then press "Show programs". The % shown is the lowest the plan accepts; a higher % gives you a better chance.
  4. Tap any card to see its details. Press "Back" to close it.
  5. Have a question? Type it in the search bar at the top, in English or Arabic.
  6. Extra details sit behind the "More info" arrow on the side, or press "Show everything". "Not confirmed" means: check with the Ministry first.
