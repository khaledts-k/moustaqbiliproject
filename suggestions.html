How to apply

The websites you will use to apply to universities, book tests and use government services.

1. University application websites

You apply to the university yourself. The Ministry scholarship pays for it once you are accepted.

Common App

United States

One website to apply to many US universities.

  • Make your account before grade 12.
  • Write one main essay of up to 650 words.
  • Ask your school counsellor and teachers for recommendation letters.
  • Sign the FERPA waiver. It keeps your teachers' letters private, which universities prefer.
commonapp.org

UCAS

United Kingdom

One website to apply to UK universities.

  • Choose up to 5 courses (up to 4 for medicine).
  • Link your account to your school with its UCAS "buzzword" (a code your school gives you).
  • Write one personal statement of up to 4,000 characters.
  • Deadlines: 15 October for medicine, dentistry, Oxford and Cambridge. Mid-January for most other courses. Check ucas.com for the exact date.
ucas.com

BridgeU / MaiaLearning

Your school

Tools some schools use to send your grades and teacher letters to universities.

  • Your school links it to Common App, so your grades are sent for you.
  • You can see when your teachers have sent their letters.
bridge-u.com

Australia and Canada

  • Australia: apply on each university's website, or through a state centre (VTAC in Victoria, UAC in New South Wales).
  • Canada: Ontario universities use OUAC. Canada is not in the Ministry's 2026/2027 scholarship plan.

2. Where to book tests

College Board (SAT)

Book the SAT and send your scores to universities.

collegeboard.org

British Council / IDP (IELTS)

Book IELTS Academic or UKVI IELTS in Kuwait. The Ministry only accepts tests taken in Kuwait.

britishcouncil.org

Pearson VUE (UCAT, LNAT)

Book the UCAT (for UK medicine) and the LNAT (for UK law).

pearsonvue.com

3. Kuwaiti government services

Kuwait Mobile ID (Hawyti)

Your digital Civil ID. You use it to log in to the scholarship registration portal.

Sahel app

The government services app. Book a Ministry appointment, request a degree equivalency, and approve the scholarship pledge (by your guardian).

sahel.paci.gov.kw

Ministry of Higher Education website

Scholarship registration, allowance tables and news.

mohe.edu.kw
