Essential Application Portals & Setup Guide

Recommended digital platforms every Kuwaiti high school student must create and link to secure university admissions and MoHE sponsorship approval.

1. International University Application Platforms

Common Application (CommonApp) United States

The centralized portal for applying to over 1,000 universities in the US (including Harvard, Stanford, NYU, Michigan, and Georgia Tech).

  • Create account in Summer before Grade 12 (opens August 1).
  • Write the 650-word Common App essay.
  • Invite your high school counselor and teachers for recommendation letters.
  • Sign the FERPA rights waiver (waiving rights shows colleges confidentiality).
commonapp.org →
UCAS Hub (Universities & Colleges) United Kingdom

The sole unified application service for all UK higher education institutions (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL, Manchester).

  • Enables selecting up to 5 university courses (max 4 for Medicine/Dentistry).
  • Requires linking with your school's UCAS Buzzword.
  • Upload 4,000-character Personal Statement.
  • Key deadlines: October 15 (Medicine/Oxbridge) | January 29 (General).
ucas.com →
BridgeU / MaiaLearning School Guidance

Used by international private schools in Kuwait to manage university shortlists, match qualifications, and transmit official transcripts.

  • Link CommonApp account with BridgeU for automatic transcript dispatch.
  • Track teacher recommendation letter submissions.
  • Explore university acceptance analytics for Kuwaiti alumni.
bridge-u.com →
Regional Portals (OUAC / VTAC / UAC) Canada & Australia

Specific centralized application systems for Ontario universities (OUAC 105) and Australian state admission centers (VTAC / UAC / QTAC).

  • OUAC 105 (Ontario, Canada): Toronto, Waterloo, McMaster.
  • VTAC & UAC (Australia): Melbourne, Monash, Sydney, UNSW.
  • Direct university applications are also accepted at most Australian institutions.
Direct Application Available at Most Canadian & Australian Faculties

2. Standardized Testing & Language Portals

College Board (Digital SAT)

Book Digital SAT exam slots at authorized test centers in Kuwait. Use code for score sending.

collegeboard.org →
British Council / IDP (IELTS)

Register for UKVI Academic IELTS or Standard Academic IELTS. Locations in Kuwait City & Hawally.

britishcouncil.org →
Pearson VUE (UCAT & LNAT)

Mandatory for UK clinical medicine (UCAT) and top law faculties (LNAT). Administered at Pearson centers in Kuwait.

pearsonvue.com →

3. State of Kuwait Mandatory Authentication Portals

Kuwait Mobile ID (تطبيق هويتي)

Digital civil identity certification used for logging into MoHE registration and verifying graduation certificates.

PACI Certified Digital ID
Sahel App (تطبيق سهل الموحد)

Request ministerial certificate equivalency (معادلة الثانوية), release civil records, and monitor scholarship updates.

Government Electronic Gateway
MoHE Portal (mohe.edu.kw)

The official portal for annual scholarship registration, financial guarantee downloads, and cultural mission file setup.
