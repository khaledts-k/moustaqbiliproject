Direct Application Guide

Recommended platforms for applying directly to universities worldwide.

1. University Application Platforms

CommonApp United States

Centralized platform for US university applications (excluding UC system).

  • Create account before Grade 12.
  • Write the 650-word personal essay.
  • Request counselor & teacher recommendation letters.
  • Complete FERPA waiver.
commonapp.org →
UCAS United Kingdom

Centralized application service for UK universities.

  • Apply to up to 5 courses (max 4 for Medicine).
  • Link with your school's UCAS Buzzword.
  • Upload your 4,000-character Personal Statement.
  • Key deadlines: 15 October (Medicine, Dentistry, Oxbridge) and mid-January for other courses. Confirm the exact date on ucas.com.
ucas.com →
BridgeU / MaiaLearning School Guidance

High school portal used to transmit official transcripts and recommendations.

  • Link CommonApp with BridgeU for automatic transcript sending.
  • Track teacher recommendation letter submissions.
bridge-u.com →
Regional Portals (OUAC / VTAC / UAC) Canada & Australia

Centralized systems for Ontario (OUAC) and Australian states (VTAC / UAC / QTAC).

  • OUAC (Ontario, Canada): Toronto, Waterloo, McMaster.
  • VTAC & UAC (Australia): Melbourne, Monash, Sydney, UNSW.
  • Direct university applications are also accepted at most Australian institutions.
