Kuwait Public & Private Universities Application Guide

Comprehensive comparison of admission requirements, high school percentage floors, and government scholarship funding across Kuwait's national institutions.

🏛️ Public Government Universities

Kuwait University (KU) and Abdullah Al-Salem University (AASU). 100% government funded for Kuwaitis with 200 KWD monthly social allowance.

Requires Kuwait University Aptitude Tests (اختبارات القدرات)
🎓 Private Universities (PUC Internal Scholarships)

AUM, GUST, AUK, KCST, and AU. Fully funded by the Ministry via the Private Universities Council (مجلس الجامعات الخاصة) with 200 KWD monthly allowance.

Minimum 70% Scientific for Engineering | 60% for Business
