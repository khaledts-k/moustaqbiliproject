Kuwait Universities

Kuwait Public & Private Universities

Admission requirements, grade floors, and scholarship options for universities in Kuwait.

Public universities

Kuwait University and Abdullah Al-Salem University. Free tuition for Kuwaiti students. Admission uses the Qudrat aptitude tests.

Private universities (PUC internal scholarships)

Tuition paid by the government at approved private universities. 2026/2027 minimums: 78% science, 80% arts, 90% engineering and law.
